# Grainfall — Support

**Stuck on a level?** After a retry or 15 seconds, tap the 💡 button to see the intended path.

**Restore your purchase:** Settings → Restore Purchases, or the Restore button on the unlock
screen. Purchases are shared with your family through Family Sharing.

**No sound?** Grainfall respects the silent switch. Also check Settings → Sound effects / Music.

**Reset progress:** Settings → Reset Progress. This keeps your purchase.

Contact: support@example.com. Please include your device model and iOS version.
