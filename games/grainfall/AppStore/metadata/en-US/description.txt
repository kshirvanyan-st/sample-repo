Every grain of sand, drop of water and blob of lava in Grainfall is simulated, pixel by pixel.

Drag your finger to dig through dirt, tap gates to release what's behind them, and guide the flow into the cup. Simple to learn — and deeply satisfying to watch.

MATERIALS THAT REACT
• Sand piles up and slides down slopes
• Water rushes sideways and finds every gap
• Lava ruins anything it touches — but water cools it into solid stone
• Plants burn, fire spreads, steam rises
• Blast charges break stone you made yourself

60 HAND-CRAFTED PUZZLES
Six chapters, each built around a new idea: First Grains, Flow, Hot Stuff, Cool Down, Overgrowth and Alchemy. Every level has a clever solution — dig less to earn all three stars.
