# Grainfall — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 2026_

Grainfall is a puzzle game made by Your Studio ("we").

**We don't collect any data.** Grainfall has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no
tracking. It contains no third-party SDKs.

- **Your progress** (stars, settings) is stored only on your device, in the app's private
  storage. It never leaves the device, and deleting the app deletes it.
- **Purchases** are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment
  details. Grainfall asks the App Store only whether the Full Game has been purchased.
- **Game Center** (optional) is provided by Apple. If you sign in, your score and achievements
  are shared with Game Center under Apple's privacy policy. We receive no personal information.
- **Sharing:** when you tap Share after a level, the image and text go only where you choose
  to send them.

Grainfall is suitable for children and collects no information from anyone, including children
under 13.

Questions: privacy@example.com
